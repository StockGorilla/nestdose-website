Nestdose vs TOM: an honest comparison

Short version. TOM (Take Your Meds) is a long-standing freemium medication reminder — free with ads interspersed between your reminders, premium to remove them {VERIFY}. Nestdose is ad-free, with no patient-tier paywall, and no banners interrupting the moment you take a pill. If you'd be fine paying TOM's premium to drop the ads anyway, Nestdose costs the same or less — $29.99/year — and that one plan covers the helpers in your circle instead of just you. If TOM's free-with-ads is a dealbreaker for you philosophically, Nestdose was built with that in mind.

The ad question

TOM's free tier historically shows banner or interstitial ads on the main dose screen {VERIFY: open TOM free on a fresh install and confirm ad behaviour on the "today" view}. For most apps, ads are the price of free. For an app you tap when it's time to take your heart medication, some people find them inappropriate — the thing that keeps you alive shouldn't share screen real estate with a car-insurance offer. That's a judgement call, not a universal rule, but it's why a free-forever, no-ads tier was written into Nestdose's product rules from the first week.

Pricing, side-by-side

The practical difference. If you're already planning to pay TOM to drop the ads ($29.99/yr Premium), you're paying the same as Nestdose's Family plan — but Nestdose's plan also covers your spouse, your daughter, and the home-aide. On TOM you'd be paying $29.99 for your own ad removal and buying each caregiver seat separately. {VERIFY}

When TOM is the better choice

  • You're on Android as the patient. TOM supports Android; Nestdose patient devices are iOS 18+ only.
  • You want drug interaction warnings. TOM includes them {VERIFY}; Nestdose does not, by design.
  • You don't mind the ads and the free tier covers everything you need — TOM's free tier is genuinely full-featured for a solo patient.
  • Your family uses a mix of Android and iPhone and everyone wants a native app. Nestdose caregivers can view via the browser, but TOM has a native Android app for everyone.

When Nestdose is the better choice

  • You don't want ads next to your dose reminder. It's a small thing until it isn't. The free tier is zero-ads, forever.
  • One family member helps manage your meds. Missed-dose alerts to their phone within minutes — covered by the single $29.99/yr subscription.
  • You care that your medication list isn't a profile on someone else's server. Nestdose has no backend. Your data is in your iCloud; the vendor cannot read it.
  • You've been quietly annoyed by streaks and adherence percentages. Nestdose shows "2 of 6", not "33%". No badges. No "you broke your streak" nudges after a rough day.

About the missing drug-interaction feature

Nestdose records what your prescriber ordered and reminds you to take it. We do not check interactions, flag side effects, or score your adherence as "safe" / "at risk" — those claims put the app in a regulated place, and the honest path is to leave clinical judgement to clinicians. Your pharmacist runs an interaction check every time they fill a prescription; if you want a second check at home, TOM, Medisafe, and Drugs.com all do that better than Nestdose would.

Switching from TOM to Nestdose

There's no automated import. Manual path:

  1. Open TOM, screenshot your medication list (name, strength, times).
  2. Install Nestdose and tap Set up for myself.
  3. Re-enter each medication: 5–10 minutes for a typical regimen of 4–8 meds.
  4. If a family member helps, tap Circle → Invite and text them the link. They tap once and they're in.
  5. Delete TOM from your phone — or keep it for the interaction checker, since Nestdose doesn't do that.

Frequently asked

Does Nestdose show ads?
No. The patient tier is free forever and ad-free; the Family plan (that caregivers pay) is what funds the product.
Can my family see my medications without their own subscription?
The Family plan covers the caregivers and viewers in your circle. The patient never pays. One subscription handles the helpers.
Will Nestdose remind me if I'm offline?
Yes. Reminders are pre-scheduled to your device — they fire without network. Nestdose keeps 64 notifications queued ahead and tops them up daily in the background.
Does Nestdose track my medication data for advertising?
No. Medication names never leave your device for analytics. Nestdose has no backend server. Data lives in your Apple iCloud (CloudKit); the vendor cannot read or sell it.
Is Nestdose on Android?
Not yet as a patient device — the reminder engine is iOS 18+ and Apple Watch. Caregivers on Android can view the patient's circle in a browser at caregiver.nestdose.com.
What if I'm using TOM's Premium already?
If what you paid Premium for is "no ads", Nestdose's free tier gets you that for $0. If what you paid Premium for is family sharing or export, Nestdose's Family plan ($29.99/yr) covers both — and covers your whole family on one subscription.

The TL;DR

TOM is the oldest-reliable freemium medication reminder app. It's full-featured, it works, and the free tier covers a solo user if they tolerate ads. Nestdose is built for people who don't want ads next to the thing that keeps them alive, and for families where one plan should cover everyone who helps. If you'd be paying TOM Premium to drop ads anyway, Nestdose costs the same and includes your family.
