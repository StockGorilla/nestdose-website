Nestdose vs Medisafe: an honest comparison

Short version. If you're a single person taking your own medications, Nestdose is free forever with no feature limits — unlimited meds, all schedule types, Apple Watch, widgets, full history, printable med list. Medisafe removed its comparable free tier in 2024 and now gates unlimited meds behind Premium {VERIFY}. If you need your family to see your schedule and get a push when a dose is missed, Nestdose charges $29.99 per year for the family plan; Medisafe charges per caregiver {VERIFY}. Pick based on whether you're alone or in a care circle, and whether a reminder app without drug-interaction warnings is a dealbreaker.

What actually changed in 2024

For years, Medisafe's selling point was a free tier that handled an entire medication regimen without limits — the thing that made it the default recommendation on r/ChronicIllness and in caregiver forums. In 2024 that tier was removed, and users who had built a habit around it hit a paywall for features that had been free the day before. {VERIFY: cite a review or Medisafe changelog that pins the exact date and scope.} The complaints in App Store reviews and r/medisafe after that shift are the reason Nestdose's free-forever promise is written into the product rules, not the marketing copy.

Pricing, side-by-side

Nestdose

  • Patient tier: free forever. Unlimited medications, every schedule type, Apple Watch, widgets, full history, printable list, HealthKit write. Nothing a solo patient uses is ever behind a paywall. This is a product rule, written into the architecture.
  • Family plan: $29.99 / year (or $3.99 / month) — the plan the caregivers pay, with a 14-day free trial. Covers the circle of helpers (up to four per circle). The person taking the medication never pays.
  • Launch / seasonal: $79.99 lifetime — offered at launch and around holidays only, never default.

Medisafe (verify at medisafe.com before relying on these numbers)

  • Free tier (pre-2024 features): removed / restricted.
  • Premium: approximately $4.99/month or $39.99/year per device {VERIFY}.
  • Caregiver seats: priced separately, per caregiver {VERIFY}.

The practical difference. A family where mom is the patient, her son is a caregiver, and a home-aide is a viewer: on Nestdose that costs $29.99/yr total. On Medisafe, approximately $5/mo × 2 caregivers + whatever the patient's Premium costs — call it $15/mo if you assume everyone pays their own tier. The math isn't close.

When Medisafe is the better choice

  • You need drug-interaction warnings. Nestdose intentionally does not offer this — we're a reminder, not a clinician. Medisafe has this built in; use it, or ask your pharmacist.
  • You need to scan a bottle to auto-fill the medication name, strength, and shape. Medisafe's camera scan is better than ours today.
  • You're already using Medisafe, your family is on it, your schedule is set up, and switching costs more than the savings over a year.
  • The patient is on Android. Nestdose patient devices are iOS 18+ only. Caregiver access on Android works through a browser; the patient side does not.

When Nestdose is the better choice

  • You're taking medications for yourself and want a reminder that stays free. Not "free trial" or "free with limits" — the patient tier has no limits and never will. That's a product rule, not a marketing position.
  • A family member helps manage your meds and needs to be alerted if a dose is missed. Nestdose's caregiver alert fires within minutes of a missed-dose window. One Family plan covers the circle.
  • You want everything on your own iCloud, not a vendor server. Nestdose has no backend. Medication names never go to analytics. Sharing happens through Apple's CloudKit — the vendor cannot read your data.
  • You're tired of streaks, percentages, and "you missed a dose" banners. Nestdose shows progress as "2 of 6", never "33%". No badges on patient screens. No upsell banners.

About the missing drug-interaction feature

Medication reminder apps that ship interaction warnings put themselves in a complicated place: FDA classification, liability for a false-negative, the ever-current database. Nestdose made a different call — be the best reminder you've ever used, and leave clinical judgement to clinicians. If interactions are a real concern, your pharmacist will check them when you fill the prescription, and a tool like Drugs.com or Medisafe does a better job than we would.

Switching from Medisafe to Nestdose

There's no automated import yet. Here's the fastest manual path:

  1. Open Medisafe on your phone, screenshot the medication list.
  2. Install Nestdose and tap Set up for myself.
  3. Add each medication: name, strength, form (tablet / capsule / etc.), schedule, refill if you track it. Most people have 4–8 meds; this takes 5–10 minutes.
  4. If a family member will help, tap Circle → Invite and send the link in Messages. They tap it once and they're in.

Frequently asked

Does Nestdose have an Android app?
Not yet. The patient device has to be an iPhone (iOS 18+) or Apple Watch. Caregivers on any platform — Android, Windows, Mac — can view the patient's circle in a browser at caregiver.nestdose.com using an emailed sign-in link.
Can Nestdose check for drug interactions?
No. We're a reminder app, not a clinical tool. Ask your pharmacist, or use Medisafe or Drugs.com for interaction checking.
What is Nestdose's family plan?
The Family plan is $29.99 per year (or $3.99 per month) and covers the people who help. The person taking the medication never pays — the plan is for the caregivers and viewers.
What happens to my data if I cancel the Family plan?
Everything stays on the patient's device. Nestdose has no backend server — your medication data lives in your iCloud (Apple CloudKit). Cancelling removes caregiver access; it does not delete your data.
Why is the person taking the medication never charged?
Reminders are a trust feature, not a paid feature. If you'd pay for the app to remind you to take your heart medication, you'd also quit the app the day a reminder didn't fire. The patient tier is complete so the circle feature — which is what caregivers actually pay for — has something to connect to.
Is there a free trial for the Family plan?
Yes, 14 days. We send a notification 2 days before the trial ends so you can cancel before you're charged.

The TL;DR

Medisafe is the richer app — interaction warnings, pill scan, longer history. Nestdose is the honest-pricing app — reminders free for life, one flat fee for the family, no shame UX, your data on your iCloud. If you want a clinical companion, use Medisafe. If you want reminders that just work and a family that can help without each paying, Nestdose is built for you.
