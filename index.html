What it looks like.

Each screen is tuned for one audience and one moment — not stuffed with everything the app can do.

Built for two people.

The person taking the medication uses Nestdose for free, always. The family plan is for the son, daughter, or helper who wants to assist from their own phone.

Private by architecture.

There is no Nestdose server. Everything lives in your iCloud private database. Family sharing uses Apple's CKShare — direct device to device. Medication names never go to analytics. No third-party trackers see what you take.

Read the privacy policy →

What Nestdose does not do.

Nestdose is a reminder and record-keeper. It does not recommend doses, suggest schedules, check drug interactions, or offer any kind of medical advice. Those decisions belong to the people you've already chosen to trust: your prescriber and your pharmacist.
