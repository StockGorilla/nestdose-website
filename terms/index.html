What Nestdose is

Nestdose is a medication reminder and record-keeping app. It lets you enter the medications a prescriber has already ordered, choose the times those doses are due, delivers local notifications at those times, and records whether each dose was taken, skipped, or missed. The app can share this record with family members you invite.

What Nestdose is not

Nestdose does not provide medical advice. It does not recommend doses, suggest schedules, check for drug-drug interactions, warn about allergies, or offer any form of clinical guidance. All content in the app is entered by you or by family members you've invited. Decisions about which medications to take, when to take them, and how much to take must be made with your prescribing provider and pharmacist. Nothing in Nestdose replaces that relationship.

Nestdose is not a medical device, a diagnostic tool, or a therapeutic intervention. It is a reminder.

Who may use Nestdose

Nestdose is for adults (18+) managing medications for themselves or for an adult family member they help. Parents or legal guardians may use Nestdose to manage medications for a child under 18 in their care; in that case the parent is the user and is responsible for the entries.

If you set up Nestdose on behalf of another adult, you must have their permission to record their medications and share their schedule with other family members.

Your account and data

Nestdose has no user accounts and no password. All of your content lives in your own iCloud private database. You can delete your data at any time by removing the app and purging the CloudKit container (see the privacy policy for exact steps).

Family sharing

When you invite a family member to a Nestdose circle, you are sharing medication information with them. You are responsible for choosing who gets access and for revoking access when it is no longer appropriate.

Each role (Owner, Helper, Viewer) grants specific capabilities as described in the app. Changing a member's role or removing them takes effect immediately on your device and propagates to their device the next time their app syncs.
